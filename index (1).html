China → Europe, the honest map

How hauls actually move from a Chinese warehouse to your door in Italy — and where the tax man waits.

The journey, stage by stage

  • Warehouse pickup — your agent (Kakobuy, Hoobuy, Sugargoo…) consolidates parcels and hands the haul to a line like ZLS, JDY or GTI.
  • First scan — the number goes live only after the first physical scan. Silence for 3–10 days is normal, not a lost haul.
  • Line-haul flight — China to an EU hub (Liège, Frankfurt, Warsaw or Milan). Line tracking goes quiet mid-flight.
  • Customs clearance — the decisive stage. Tax-free lines clear in bulk; standard lines get assessed item by item.
  • Last mile — handed to Poste Italiane / SDA in Italy, DPD, GLS, Colissimo, Evri… A new local number may replace the Chinese one.

Tax: the Italy numbers FOCUS

Italy applies EU rules with an Italian twist: the €150 duty threshold comes from Brussels, but IVA is charged on everything imported, even a €5 haul.

  • IVA (VAT) 22% — due on the full value of any parcel, no minimum. Since July 2021 there is no VAT-free import into Italy.
  • Customs duty — only if goods value > €150. Clothing/shoes ≈ 12%, electronics 0–6%.
  • €22 myth — dead since 2021. If a seller promises "no tax under €22", that is outdated.
  • Clearing fee — Poste ~€2 for paperwork on taxed parcels; express couriers €10–15.
  • "Tax included" lines (DDP, tax-free, double-clearance) — the line pays VAT in bulk; you pay nothing at the door, but the quote is 30–60% higher.
  • "No tax" lines (DAP) — cheaper per kg, but Poste may hold your haul until you pay 22% IVA + fees.

Same maths across Europe EU

Everywhere in the EU the structure is identical — only the VAT rate and local quirks change:

  • Germany 19% VAT, notoriously strict customs on replicas.
  • France 20% VAT; Colis Privé / La Poste last mile.
  • Spain 21% VAT; Correos clears fast for small parcels.
  • Netherlands / Belgium 21% VAT; Liège & Amsterdam are the main air hubs — often the fastest door.
  • Outside the EU — UK 20% VAT + its own £135 rule; Switzerland clears at 8.1%; the US stays the easy one: no federal import tax under $800.

Answers

Questions every hauler asks

My tracking number shows nothing anywhere. Is the haul lost?
Almost never. The number only goes live after the first physical scan at the line's warehouse — that regularly takes 3–10 days after the agent marks it "shipped". During Chinese holidays (Golden Week, CNY) add another week. Wait, rescan, and only open a ticket with your agent after ~14 days of silence.
Why did the number change halfway?
Your haul travels as a master shipment (the line's own number) that gets split into a local leg inside Europe. When ZLS or JDY hands the parcel to Poste Italiane, DPD or GLS, a new number is born. The scanner flags this: it shows the Chinese leg, and the carrier card links you to the local courier to continue.
Tax-free or tax-included line — which do I pick?
Rule of thumb for Italy: haul value under ~€130 → the cheap "no tax" line is usually fine, worst case you pay 22% IVA. Over €130 → do the maths with the calculator above; the tax-included (DDP) line often wins once you add duty + fees + the risk of the parcel sitting in customs. High-value hauls in Italy are frequently seized and reassessed.
How does the live scan actually work?
m1L runs entirely in your browser. As you type, the number's format is detected live and the matching carrier is probed first. On scan, each carrier is queried in parallel: through the carrier's own public API where available (Cainiao's global endpoint powers the live statuses you see), then through public CORS relays for the China-line portals. Every result also carries a one-click deep link with your number pre-filled, so even if a portal blocks automated queries you land directly on the result. No account, no stored numbers, nothing leaves your device except the lookup itself.
Which carrier is this number format?
Fast tells: starts YT+13 digits → Yun Express · LP → Cainiao/AliExpress · RR…CN → China Post registered · AT…CN → EMS · 10 digits → likely DHL Express · JDY prefix → Jindouyun. The detector shows the match live under the search bar while you type.
Does it work for DHL, FedEx and UPS too?
Yes — the scan chain includes DHL Express, FedEx, UPS, GLS, DPD and the postal services. Where a courier blocks direct automated queries, the one-click deep link takes you to its official tracking page with the number already typed in.
